Breast augmentation with fat transfer · Garden City / Boise

Your volume back — without an implant.

Natural Feel. Natural Look.

At Idaho Regenerative Medicine, Dr. Sara Jo Walton offers natural breast augmentation in Boise and Garden City with your own fat — breast fat grafting instead of implants. PRP is placed into each breast with the graft to support cell viability. Still your fat. Still no implant. After pregnancy, aging, or weight loss — including fat transfer after Ozempic or another GLP-1 — many women want softness back in the upper pole, not a different body. Fat taken from the abdomen, flanks, arms, back, or inner or outer thighs can contour those areas at the same time.

Fit

Who should wait.

Weight still moving

After a GLP-1 or a large weight change, wait until the scale has settled. Living fat follows your weight.

Nicotine

Nicotine works against the graft. We will be direct about that at the consult.

Not enough fat to spare

If there is not enough donor fat, we plan a smaller change or we say this is not the right procedure.

A lift or a three-cup jump

We do not lift the breast. We fill it. One session is not an A-to-D.

Your path to breast augmentation

A conversation first. A breast plan when you are ready.

01

Complimentary Get to Know You Consult

See if this is the right procedure for you. You leave with a planning range for breast fat transfer — not an individualized price. We have not examined your donor fat or the breast. This visit is fit.

02

The planning visit

This starts with a visit — a New Patient Visit if you are new here, or a follow-up if you already receive care. We assess the breast and the adipose, image the fat, take pre-procedure photos, and map where we fill. Then you leave with a plan and an individualized price estimate. That visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward.

03

Procedure day

Typically 3 or more hours, depending on harvest sites and volume transferred. You stay awake. Fat is harvested, prepared, and grafted. Platelet-rich plasma and platelet-pure plasma are placed into each breast with the fat. Home the same day with garments and written aftercare.

04

What is included after

Early follow-up, later visit at about six months, and a way to reach the clinic if something does not feel right. A second session is planned after settling if you want more cup volume.

What to expect

Your breasts, procedure day through six months.

During

Harvest and the breast

You stay awake. Typically 3 or more hours. Fat is harvested where you have extra, prepared in a closed Cellmyx system, and placed where that breast needs it.

PRP with the graft. Every breast fat-transfer session includes PRP into each breast. Extra stem-cell processing beyond PRP, if it fits your case, is decided at the New Patient Visit or a follow-up visit if you are already a patient.

Home

Going home

Someone drives you. Sleep on your back. You can reach the clinic if something does not feel right.

After

Protect the breast graft

Compression on the harvest site. No pressure on the breast until we clear underwire.

Settling

Week one is not your cup size

The result you live in shows at about six months.

Questions

What people ask before breast fat transfer.

Is PRP included?

Yes. PRP is placed into each breast with the fat graft to support viability. That is part of the procedure.

Can you add stem cells beyond PRP?

Sometimes. That is decided at the New Patient Visit — or a follow-up visit if you are already a patient — not from a photo, and not assumed.

How much does breast fat transfer cost?

The complimentary consult includes a planning range. An individualized price is built at the New Patient Visit — or at a follow-up visit if you are already a patient. That visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward.

Will fat transfer lift my breasts?

We do not lift the breast. We fill it.

Are you awake?

Yes. This is done awake at Idaho Regenerative Medicine.
