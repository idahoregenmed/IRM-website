Breast Augmentation · Garden City / Boise

Your volume back — without an implant.

Natural Feel. Natural Look.

Dr. Sara Jo Walton uses your own fat to restore breast shape after pregnancy, weight change, or years of feeling deflated. Softness that moves with you. Contour where the fat comes from. Done awake. Treasure Valley patients who want their own tissue — not a device.

Your own fat. Still you.

Half to one cup in a session is a beautiful, natural step. Complimentary consult for fit and a broad range. Your individualized quote comes after the New Patient Visit.

Who this is for

You might recognize yourself here.

Best when you have donor fat to spare, your weight is stable, and you are not using nicotine. The change is modest and natural — not a two-cup jump in one session.

Volume left after life changed

Pregnancy, nursing, aging, or weight loss — including after GLP-1 medications — can leave the breast looking deflated while you feel more like yourself everywhere else. Fat transfer moves living tissue back to where you miss it.

Your own tissue. Not an implant.

Softness that moves with you. Nothing manufactured left in the body. For people who never wanted implants, and for people who are done with them.

Contour and volume together

The harvest is the other half: a little less at the abdomen, flanks, or thighs, and a little more in the breast. Two-for-one when you have extra to spare.

A natural step, not a new silhouette

Many people land in the half-to-one-cup range per session. If you want more after settling, a second session is planned — not promised on day one.

Your path

A conversation first. A plan when you are ready.

01

Get to Know You Consult

Complimentary. You leave with the broad range that applies to everyone considering fat transfer here — not your personal quote. We have not examined your donor fat or the breast yet. This visit is fit: what you want to see, how the procedure works, and whether this practice feels like home.

02

New Patient Visit — 60 minutes

The visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward. We examine and image the fat that would be harvested, assess the breast, and review your health. Then you get the individualized plan and quote — your number, built around your body.

03

Procedure day

You come to Garden City with a ride home. You stay awake. We mark harvest and placement together. Fat is taken from an area that has extra, prepared in the closed Cellmyx AcquiCell and Lipo-Loop system, and placed in the breast. Comfort is planned with you. Home the same day with garments and written aftercare.

04

What is included after

Early follow-up checks harvest sites, the graft, garments, and activity. A later visit looks at the settled result — about six months. If you want more volume, that is when a second session is planned. No pressure on treated breasts. You can reach the clinic if something does not feel right.

How this compares

Fat transfer vs breast implants.

Vs implants

Not a device

Implants add a manufactured size. Fat transfer adds your own tissue. Large, immediate jumps are what implants do. This is for people who want to look like themselves.

Scars

Small access points

Not a breast-fold implant scar. The marks from harvest and placement usually fade.

Two-for-one

Contour plus volume

Abdomen, flanks, or thighs can look more refined while the breast gains living volume.

Feel

Softness that is yours

What lives is your fat. It will follow your weight. That is the point — and the limit.

What to expect

Procedure day through settling.

During

Harvest and placement

You stay awake. Fat is harvested, prepared in a closed Cellmyx system, and placed in the breast. Research on this platform reports about 90% cell viability.

Home

Going home

Someone drives you. Garments and aftercare leave with you. Sleep so nothing presses on the breast. Harvest sites feel bruised; that is expected. You can reach the clinic if something does not feel right.

After

Protect the graft

Compression belongs on the harvest site. Pressure does not belong on the breast. Walking is usually encouraged. Impact and heavy lifting wait.

Settling

Week one is not the result

Swelling makes it look bigger at first. Fluid leaves. Living fat remains. The result you live in shows after settling — about six months.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much bigger can I get in one session?

A beautiful, modest step. Many people are in the half-to-one-cup range per session. A second session can be planned after settling.

How much of the fat stays?

Research on the AcquiCell Lipo-Loop system reports about 90% cell viability. Week-one size is still swelling. Settling takes about six months. What lives will follow your weight.

Are you awake?

Yes. Breast fat transfer here is done awake. Comfort is planned with you before procedure day.

Does it hurt? When can I work?

Harvest sites are usually more tender than the breast. Many people return to desk work within a few days if they can protect the graft.

Can I still get mammograms?

Yes. Tell the imaging team. Fat grafting does not replace screening.

What if I do not have much extra fat?

We plan around what you have. If there is not enough donor fat, or if weight or nicotine would work against the graft, we will say so at the consult.

How much does it cost?

The complimentary consult can include a broad range. Your individualized quote is built at the 60-minute New Patient Visit. The visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward.
