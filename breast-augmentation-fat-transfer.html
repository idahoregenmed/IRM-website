Who should wait.

This works best when donor fat is there, weight is stable, and nicotine is not in the way. We will say so at the consult if the timing is wrong.

Weight still moving

After a GLP-1 or a large weight change, wait until the scale has settled. Living fat follows your weight. If you are still losing, the breasts will too.

Nicotine

Nicotine works against the graft. If you use it, this is not the week to book procedure day. We will be direct about that at the consult.

Not enough fat to spare

The harvest has to come from somewhere. If there is not enough donor fat, we plan a smaller change, a different site, or we say this is not the right procedure.

A lift or a three-cup jump

We do not lift the breast. We fill it. One session is not an A-to-D. If that is the only outcome that would feel like success, implants or a surgical lift belong in a different office.

Before you decide

What you need to know.

Size, feel, harvest sites, bras, imaging, and what this will not do. Straight answers for women considering breast augmentation with their own fat.

Will I look like I had work?

Clothes should fit better. A T-shirt should look like you. If the goal is a high, round, obviously augmented upper pole, that is an implant conversation — and we will say so.

How many cup sizes is real?

Most sessions add 0.5 to 1.5 cups. Many people plan two sessions on purpose. Week one looks bigger because of swelling. Settling takes about six months.

What about after Ozempic or pregnancy?

This is one of the main reasons women come in. Fat transfer can restore softness if you still have donor fat and your weight is stable. We fill where that breast needs it. We do not lift the breast. We fill it.

Can I take fat from my stomach and fill the bra?

Yes — and not only the stomach. Abdomen, flanks, arms, back, and inner or outer thighs. Confirmed at the New Patient Visit, not from a photo.

What bra do I wear after?

Soft support. No underwire and no tight sports bra that flattens the upper pole until we clear it. Sleep on your back.

Will this mess up mammograms?

Tell every imaging team. Screening still happens. Small oil cysts or calcifications can show; readers used to fat grafting usually recognize them.

Questions

What people ask before breast fat transfer.

Will fat transfer lift my breasts?

We do not lift the breast. We fill it. Filling behind the nipple and the lower pole can take a nipple that points down and make it look perkier. Skin is not removed.

How much bigger can my breasts get in one session?

Most sessions add 0.5 to 1.5 cups. Many people plan two sessions on purpose. The second is discussed after the breasts settle — about six months.

Where does the fat come from?

Abdomen, flanks, arms, back, and inner or outer thighs. Chosen at the New Patient Visit from the fat you actually have.

How much of the fat stays in the breast?

Research on the AcquiCell Lipo-Loop system reports about 90% cell viability. Week-one size is still swelling. Settling takes about six months.

Can you add stem cells to the fat transfer?

Yes. Decided at the New Patient Visit. Stem cells can support viability and the nipple.

How long is procedure day?

Typically 3 or more hours. Timing depends on grafting sites and how much volume is transferred.

Can I breastfeed after fat transfer?

Many people can. We cannot promise it. If you hope to nurse later, say so at the consult.

I already have implants. Can I still do this?

Fat transfer is not implant removal. After implants are out, your own fat can restore softness. Decided in person.

Are you awake?

Yes. Breast augmentation with fat transfer at Idaho Regenerative Medicine is done awake.

When can I wear a regular bra?

Not at first. Soft support only. We will tell you when underwire is safe. Many people return to desk work within a few days if they can protect the breast.

Can I still get mammograms?

Yes. Tell the imaging team. Screening still happens. Small oil cysts or calcifications can show; readers used to fat grafting usually recognize them.

What if I do not have much extra fat?

The plan is built around what you have. If there is not enough donor fat, or if weight or nicotine would work against the graft, we will say so at the consult.

How much does breast fat transfer cost?

The complimentary consult includes a price estimate. An individualized price estimate is built at the New Patient Visit. The visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward.
