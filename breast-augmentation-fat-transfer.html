Your path to breast augmentation

A conversation first. A breast plan when you are ready.

01

Get to Know You Consult

Complimentary. You leave with a price estimate for breast fat transfer — not an individualized price yet. We have not examined your donor fat or the breast. This visit is fit: the size you hope for, whether volume alone matches what you see, and whether this practice feels like home.

02

New Patient Visit — 60 minutes

The visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward. We examine and image the fat that would be harvested, assess the breast — skin, symmetry, lower pole, behind the nipple, upper pole, prior surgery or imaging — and review your health. Where we fill is mapped here. Harvest can come from the abdomen, flanks, arms, back, or inner or outer thighs. Stem cells can be added to the graft to support viability and the nipple. Then you get the individualized breast plan and price estimate.

03

Procedure day

You come to Garden City with a ride home. You stay awake. We mark the harvest site and the breast together. Fat is prepared in the closed Cellmyx AcquiCell and Lipo-Loop system and placed in the breast so the graft can live. Comfort is planned with you. Home the same day with garments and written aftercare for the breast and the harvest site.

04

What is included after

Early follow-up checks the harvest sites and the breasts, garments, and activity. No pressure on treated breasts — sleep on your back, no underwire until we say so. A later visit looks at the settled breast — about six months. If you want more cup volume, that is when a second session is planned. You can reach the clinic if something does not feel right.

What people actually ask

The questions behind the videos.

On TikTok and Instagram the nickname is natural boob job. Underneath it, women ask the same few things. Here is how we answer them in Garden City.

Will I look like I had work?

That is the point of fat transfer. Clothes should fit better. A T-shirt should look like you. Friends should not clock a device. If the goal is a high, round, obviously augmented upper pole, that is an implant conversation — and we will say so.

How many cup sizes is real?

Videos skip this. A conservative increase is 0.5 to 1.5 cups per session. Week one looks bigger because of swelling. Settling takes about six months. A second session is a choice after that — not a surprise we hide.

What about after Ozempic or pregnancy?

This is one of the main reasons women come in. The body changed. The bra did not keep up. Fat transfer can restore softness if you still have donor fat and your weight is stable. We fill where that breast needs it — lower pole, behind the nipple, upper pole. Filling behind the nipple and the lower pole can take a nipple that points down and make it look perkier. We do not lift the breast. We fill it.

Can I take fat from my stomach and fill the bra?

Yes — and not only the stomach. We can contour the abdomen, flanks, arms, back, and inner or outer thighs while the breasts gain living volume. You need enough to spare. We confirm that at the New Patient Visit, not from a photo.

What bra do I wear after?

Soft support. No underwire and no tight sports bra that flattens the upper pole until we clear it. Sleep on your back. Compression belongs on the harvest site, not smashed onto the graft.

Will this mess up mammograms?

Tell every imaging team you have had fat transfer to the breast. Screening still matters. Calcifications or small oil cysts can show; experienced readers usually know the difference. Keep getting screened.

What to expect

Your breasts, procedure day through six months.

During

Harvest and the breast

You stay awake. Fat is harvested from the abdomen, flanks, arms, back, or inner or outer thighs — wherever you have extra — prepared in a closed Cellmyx system, and placed where that breast needs it. Research on this platform reports about 90% cell viability. How much volume you keep is judged after settling — not at week one.

Home

Going home

Someone drives you. A supportive garment and written aftercare leave with you. Sleep on your back so nothing presses on the breast. Harvest sites feel bruised; that is expected. You can reach the clinic if something does not feel right.

After

Protect the breast graft

Compression belongs on the harvest site. Pressure does not belong on the breast — no sleeping on your stomach, no underwire, no tight sports bra that flattens the upper pole until we clear it. Walking is usually fine. Impact and heavy lifting wait.

Settling

Week one is not your cup size

Swelling makes the breasts look bigger at first. Fluid leaves. Living fat remains. The breasts you live in show after settling — about six months. That is when we talk about a second session if you want more.

Questions

What people ask before breast fat transfer.

Will fat transfer lift my breasts?

We do not lift the breast. We fill it where it needs volume — lower pole, behind the nipple, upper pole, or a combination. That map is made at the New Patient Visit. Filling behind the nipple and the lower pole can take a nipple that points down and make it look perkier. Skin is not removed and the nipple is not relocated the way a lift would.

How much bigger can my breasts get in one session?

A conservative increase is 0.5 to 1.5 cups per session. Going from an A to a D in one day is not what this procedure does. A second session can be planned after the breasts settle.

Where does the fat come from?

We can contour the abdomen, flanks, arms, back, and inner or outer thighs. The harvest site is chosen at the New Patient Visit from the fat you actually have.

How much of the fat stays in the breast?

Research on the AcquiCell Lipo-Loop system reports about 90% cell viability. Week-one size is still swelling. Settling takes about six months. What lives in the breast will follow your weight.

Can you add stem cells to the fat transfer?

Yes. Stem cells can be added to the graft to help support cell viability and the settled outcome — including the nipple. Whether that belongs in your plan is decided at the New Patient Visit.

How long is procedure day?

Plan on a few hours in Garden City. Exact timing depends on how many harvest sites we use and how much fat is placed in the breast. That is set at the New Patient Visit.

Can I breastfeed after fat transfer?

Many people can. We cannot promise it. If you hope to nurse later, say so at the consult so we can plan placement with that in mind.

I already have implants. Can I still do this?

Fat transfer is not implant removal. After implants are out — or if you never wanted them — your own fat can restore softness so you are not left empty. Whether that sequence is right is decided in person.

Are you awake?

Yes. Breast augmentation with fat transfer at Idaho Regenerative Medicine is done awake. Comfort is planned with you before procedure day.

When can I wear a regular bra?

Not at first. The graft needs no pressure on the breast. We will tell you when underwire and a tight sports bra are safe again. Many people return to desk work within a few days if they can protect the breast.

Can I still get mammograms?

Yes. Tell the imaging team you have had fat transfer to the breast. It does not replace screening.

What if I do not have much extra fat?

The breast plan is built around what you have. If there is not enough donor fat, or if weight or nicotine would work against the graft, we will say so at the consult.

How much does breast fat transfer cost?

The complimentary consult includes a price estimate. An individualized price estimate is built at the 60-minute New Patient Visit. The visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward.
