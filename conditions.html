How to know

When it may be time for a different lens.

You do not need to arrive with the perfect diagnosis or know which therapy you need. If you want someone to connect the dots and build a plan around your whole body, this is the right place to begin.

You keep treating one piece at a time

You may have seen specialists, tried protocols, or managed symptoms separately, but still feel like no one has connected pain, energy, hormones, metabolism, stress, and recovery into one plan.

Your labs or imaging do not explain how you feel

You may be told everything looks “normal,” yet you know your body is not performing, healing, sleeping, moving, or responding the way it used to. Sometimes the next step is deeper assessment of gut health, toxins, mold, heavy metals, hormones, nutrients, immune patterns, inflammation, metabolism, genetics, cardiovascular risk, cellular health, Lyme, or co-infections.

You want regenerative options with clinical judgment

You may be curious about biologics, peptides, IV therapy, hormones, EBOO, shockwave, aesthetics, or orthopedic treatments, but you want guidance on what actually fits your body and goals.

You want to feel strong in the next chapter

You may be thinking beyond symptom relief toward healthspan, confidence, mobility, metabolism, skin health, recovery, cognition, and the ability to stay active in your life.

Questions

What patients often want to know.

Can you help if I have more than one concern?

Yes. Many patients come in with overlapping issues: pain plus fatigue, hormones plus weight resistance, inflammation plus stress, or recovery problems that affect several areas of life. The goal is to understand how the patterns connect.

What makes IRM an integrative regenerative practice?

IRM brings together naturopathic medicine, regenerative orthopedics, biologics, peptides, hormones, IV Therapy, EBOO, shockwave, regenerative aesthetics, and longevity-focused care. The plan is built around the person, not around one therapy.

Do I need to know which therapy I want?

No. You do not need to choose a therapy before you arrive. The conversation helps clarify whether your best next step is assessment, foundational repair, regenerative treatment, metabolic support, hormone work, aesthetics, or another direction.

How do you decide where to start?

We start with your story, goals, current symptoms, prior care, exam findings, and the systems most likely to be limiting your repair capacity. For musculoskeletal concerns, in-office ultrasound may help clarify the tissue picture. When the whole-body picture is incomplete, additional assessment may help clarify what to address first.

Is this only for people with injuries?

No. Injuries and joint concerns are part of the practice, but IRM also supports patients seeking help with hormones, metabolism, immune patterns, stress physiology, longevity, skin and hair regeneration, and whole-body resilience.
