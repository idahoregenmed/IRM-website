Guide

Understanding regenerative medicine at IRM

Regenerative care here is not a single injection or protocol. It is a sequence: listen for the pattern, support the terrain, regenerate tissue when ready, and optimize longevity over time. Read our four-phase framework →

Guide

Why sequence matters more than the trendiest therapy

Many patients arrive asking about PRP, stem cells, or peptides first. At IRM, the question is what your body needs next — which may be assessment, terrain support, or nervous system regulation before a targeted therapy. Explore the therapy toolkit →

Guide

What "normal labs" can miss

Standard blood work is valuable — but it does not always capture early inflammation, nutrient gaps, gut-immune patterns, hormone rhythm issues, or tissue-level problems driving symptoms. See how IRM assesses →
