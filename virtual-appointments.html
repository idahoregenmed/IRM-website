Virtual care

Virtual appointments when distance or schedule makes in-person difficult.

Idaho Regenerative Medicine offers telehealth visits for new patient consults, follow-ups, care planning, lab reviews, and ongoing support — so you can connect with Dr. Walton and the IRM team from home.

Care that meets you where you are.

Whether you live outside the Treasure Valley, travel frequently, or simply prefer the convenience of a virtual visit, telehealth keeps you connected to your care team.

Visit types

When virtual appointments make sense.

New patients

Get to Know You Consult

A relationship-first conversation to hear your story, clarify goals, and decide whether IRM is the right fit — available virtually when appropriate.

Established patients

Follow-up & care planning

Review progress, adjust your plan, discuss lab results, and coordinate next steps without an office visit.

Assessment

Lab & record review

Discuss prior labs, imaging, and records to help map the pattern and prioritize what information may still be needed.

Ongoing support

Check-ins & coordination

Stay connected between in-person visits for questions, medication adjustments, and care continuity.

What requires in-person

Some care is best done at the clinic.

Regenerative injection therapies, in-office ultrasound, live blood analysis, IV therapy, ozone treatments, and physical exams require an in-person visit at our Garden City clinic.

Your provider will clarify which visits can be virtual and which need to happen in person. Many patients combine telehealth consults with periodic in-clinic treatments. View our clinic location.

How do I book a virtual appointment?

Book a telehealth visit through our online scheduling portal or call 208-370-2380. You can also view all options on our scheduling page.

What technology do I need for a telehealth visit?

You will need a device with a camera and microphone (phone, tablet, or computer) and a stable internet connection. Secure video link details are provided after booking.

Can new patients start with a virtual consult?

Yes. The Get to Know You Consult is available virtually for many new patients. Learn about becoming a new patient.

Do you offer telehealth outside of Idaho?

Telehealth availability depends on licensing and clinical appropriateness. Contact the office at info@idahoregenmed.com to confirm eligibility for your location.
