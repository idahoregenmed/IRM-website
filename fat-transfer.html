Fat transfer that uses your own tissue.

At Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM) in Garden City near Boise, fat transfer means harvesting your own fat and placing it where volume or contour is wanted. This category is for breast and gluteal augmentation. Facial MFAT biofiller is featured under Regenerative Aesthetics.

Book NowBreast fat transfer

Living fat, not an implant.

IRM uses a closed-loop harvest and transfer approach with Cellmyx AcquiCell and Lipo-Loop so fat can be taken from one area and placed in another — when you have enough donor tissue and a realistic goal.

The IRM lens

Fat is living tissue, not a product off a shelf.

Autologous fat transfer harvests fat from areas with extra volume, prepares it, and places it where more shape is wanted. Some of the transferred fat is expected to resorb. That is biology, not failure.

Graft survival depends on harvest quality, handling, placement, blood supply, nicotine, weight stability, hormones, nutrition, and how you heal.

Harvest

Donor-site contouring

Abdomen, flanks, and thighs are common harvest sites. The harvest plan is part of the conversation.

How we transfer

Closed-loop harvest for volume procedures.

For breast and gluteal work, IRM uses Cellmyx AcquiCell with Lipo-Loop. Facial work uses MFAT with intelliFat ReNU, which is why the face belongs on Regenerative Aesthetics.

Is this the same as filler or implants?

No. Implants add a device. Gel fillers are manufactured products. Fat transfer uses your tissue.

Will all of the fat stay?

No. Early swelling is not the final result. A portion of transferred fat typically does not survive.
