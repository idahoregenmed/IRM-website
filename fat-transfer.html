Who this is for

You might recognize yourself here.

Volume that left after life changed. Your own tissue instead of an implant. Contour and volume in the same procedure. Face and hands that no longer match how alive you feel. This works best when you have donor fat to spare, your weight is stable, and you are not using nicotine.

Volume left after life changed

Pregnancy, nursing, aging, or significant weight loss — including after GLP-1 medications — can leave breasts, hips, face, or hands looking deflated while the rest of you feels more like yourself. Fat transfer moves living tissue back to where you miss it. The change is modest and natural: the version of you that used to fill a dress, not a different person.

Your own tissue. Not an implant.

You want softness that moves with you and nothing manufactured left in the body. No device to maintain or replace. Fat transfer is for people who would rather add their own fat than place silicone or saline — including those who never wanted implants, and those who are done with them.

Contour and volume in one procedure

The fat has to come from somewhere. That harvest is the other half patients ask for: a little less at the abdomen, flanks, or thighs, and a little more where you want shape. Two-for-one when you have extra to spare. That is why people search fat transfer instead of implants alone.

The face and hands no longer match

Cheeks, temples, and hands lose padding long before you feel old — and weight loss can hollow them further. A finer preparation of your own fat can restore that cushion so people say you look well, and so your hands match the vitality you still feel. Small areas. Real difference. Still your tissue.

Your path

A conversation first. A plan when you are ready.

01

Get to Know You Consult

Complimentary, and you do leave with a number that day — the broad range that applies to everyone considering fat transfer here. That is not your personal quote. We have not examined your donor fat or the placement area yet. This visit is the conversation: what you want to see, how the procedure works at IRM, whether this practice feels like home, and enough cost context to decide if the next visit is worth it. No exam. No obligation.

02

New Patient Visit — 60 minutes

The visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward. This is the pre-procedure visit. We examine and image the fat that would be harvested, assess the area that would receive it, and review your health so procedure day is safe and specific. Then you get the individualized plan and quote — your number, built around your body, not the range from the first conversation.

03

Procedure day

You come to the Garden City clinic with a ride home. You stay awake. We review the plan and mark the harvest site and the placement area together so nothing is a surprise. Fat is taken from an area that has extra — often the abdomen, flanks, or thighs — then prepared in the closed Cellmyx AcquiCell and Lipo-Loop system so as much of that tissue as possible stays viable. That fat is placed where you want volume. Comfort is planned with you before the day starts. When the work is finished you leave with garments, written aftercare, and a clear picture of what the first days should feel like. Home the same day.

04

What is included after

You are not dropped at the door. Aftercare instructions and garments go with you. You have a way to reach the clinic if something does not feel right. An early follow-up checks healing at the harvest sites and the graft, garments, and activity. A later visit looks at the settled result — about six months, after swelling has left and the living fat has declared what stays. If you want more volume, that is when a second session is planned. Protecting the graft (no pressure on treated breasts or a fresh glute) is part of the plan we send you home with, not something you have to invent.

How this compares

Fat transfer vs implants, BBL, or filler.

Vs implants

Fat transfer vs breast implants

Implants add a manufactured size. Fat transfer adds your own tissue — softer change, nothing to replace later. Large, immediate size jumps are what implants do. This is for people who want to look like themselves.

Vs BBL surgery

Fat transfer vs a hospital BBL

Large-volume gluteal surgery in an operating room is a different operation — different anesthesia, different volumes, different risk. Here the work is thoughtful contour with your own fat, done awake.

Vs filler

Fat transfer vs gel filler

Gel can be dissolved. Fat transfer uses your tissue. On the face it is prepared more finely. What lives is yours.

Two-for-one

Contour plus volume

The harvest is liposuction with a purpose. Abdomen, flanks, or thighs can look more refined while the breast, glute, face, or hands gain volume.

What to expect

Procedure day through settling.

During

Harvest and placement

You stay awake. Fat is taken from an area that has extra — often abdomen, flanks, or thighs — prepared in a closed Cellmyx system, and placed where you want volume. Comfort is planned with you. Research on this platform reports about 90% cell viability.

Home

Going home

Someone drives you. Garments and written aftercare leave with you. Sleep so nothing presses on the graft. Harvest sites feel bruised; that is expected. You can reach the clinic if something does not feel right.

After

Protect the graft

Harvest sites feel like a deep bruise. Compression belongs there. Pressure does not belong on the graft — no sleeping on treated breasts, no sitting hard on a fresh glute graft. Walking is usually encouraged. Impact and heavy lifting wait.

Settling

Week one is not the result

Swelling makes it look bigger at first. Friends may think it is done. Fluid leaves. Living fat remains. The result you live in shows after settling — about six months.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much bigger can I get in one session?

A beautiful, modest step. For the breast, many people are in the half-to-one-cup range per session. Face and hands use smaller volumes on purpose. The glute is shaped from the fat you actually have.

How much of the fat stays?

Research on the AcquiCell Lipo-Loop system reports about 90% cell viability. Week-one size is still swelling. Settling takes about six months. What lives is living fat and will follow your weight.

Are you awake?

Yes. Fat transfer here is done awake. Comfort is planned with you before procedure day.

Does it hurt? When can I work?

Harvest sites are usually more tender than the area that received fat. Many people return to desk work within a few days if they can protect the graft. Full exercise waits longer.

Will I have scars?

Small access points — not a breast-fold implant scar. They usually fade.

What if I do not have much extra fat?

We plan around what you have: a smaller change, a different harvest site, or a staged approach. If there is not enough donor fat, or if weight or nicotine would work against the graft, we will say so at the consult.

Can I still get mammograms after breast fat transfer?

Yes. Tell the imaging team. Fat grafting does not replace screening.

How much does it cost?

The complimentary consult can include a broad range that applies to everyone considering fat transfer. Your individualized quote is built at the 60-minute New Patient Visit (the visit fee is applied to the procedure if you move forward).
