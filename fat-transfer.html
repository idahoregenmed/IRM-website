Fat transfer

Volume that still feels like you.

Fat transfer at Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM) uses your own fat to restore shape where volume has changed — after pregnancy, weight shifts, aging, or simply because you want a softer, more natural contour. The goal is not a dramatic, one-size look. The goal is fullness that belongs to your body.

Book Now Explore breast fat transfer

Your tissue. Your contour.

We harvest fat from an area that has extra, prepare it carefully, and place it where you want more shape. Breast and glute live here. Facial MFAT biofiller is featured under Regenerative Aesthetics because the face uses a finer preparation.

The IRM lens

Fat is living tissue, not a shortcut.

When people say they want to “look like themselves again,” they are often talking about volume that left after nursing, weight change, or years of living in a body that redistributed fat in ways they did not choose. Fat transfer is one way to move your own tissue instead of placing a device or a manufactured gel.

That only works if the fat is treated as living graft. How it is harvested, how gently it is handled, where it is placed, and whether the body has enough blood supply, nutrition, and recovery capacity all influence how much of that fat stays. Some resorption is expected. Early swelling is not the final result.

Is this the same as breast implants or a “BBL package”?

No. Implants add a device. Large packaged BBL language often promises a look this clinic will not sell. Fat transfer here is individualized grafting for natural breast or gluteal contour — and only when donor fat and goals line up.

How much change is realistic?

Think in terms of restoration and modest enhancement, not a jump that ignores anatomy. Available donor fat, tissue quality, and graft survival all set a ceiling. A second session is sometimes part of an honest plan.

Where fat transfer may fit

A biology-first path for natural volume and contour.

This category is for structural fat grafting to the breast and glute. Facial work uses micro-fragmented fat and belongs with Regenerative Aesthetics.

Harvest

The donor site is part of the result

Fat usually comes from the abdomen, flanks, or thighs. Patients often care as much about what is refined there as what is added to the breast or glute. Both sides of the plan get discussed.

What to expect

For patients exploring fat transfer.

This is a procedure conversation, not a product menu. The sequence is consult, candidacy, then a plan that includes harvest, transfer, and recovery.

Before

Consult and candidacy

We talk about the change you hope to see, photos if they help, where extra fat lives, nicotine, medications, prior surgery or imaging, weight stability, and whether the body needs support before grafting.

During

Harvest and placement

For breast and glute, IRM uses Cellmyx AcquiCell with Lipo-Loop — a closed system so fat can be harvested and transferred as living tissue. Comfort measures depend on the individual plan.

After

Healing and graft take

Swelling, bruising, and soreness at harvest and transfer sites are common. Compression on donor areas, avoiding pressure on the grafted site, and activity limits protect early graft survival.

Sequence

Settling and staging

Not all transferred fat remains. The result you see in the first weeks is not the result you keep. A second session may be discussed after the first graft has settled.

Patients often say

“I want to look like myself — just more restored.”

I do not want implants, but I miss the volume I used to have.

That is a common reason people ask about fat transfer to the breast. The next question is whether you have enough donor fat and whether a modest, natural change matches what you hope to see in the mirror.

Can you take fat from my abdomen or flanks and use it where I want shape?

That is the usual idea: harvest where there is extra, transfer where volume is wanted. Exact sites depend on your anatomy and what can be harvested safely.

Will all of the fat stay?

No. A portion typically does not survive. That is expected biology. Your provider will talk about resorption and whether staging makes sense before anyone is scheduled.

What about my face?

Facial volume uses MFAT — a finer, washed and sized preparation with intelliFat ReNU — not the same large-volume graft used for breast or glute. That care is featured under Regenerative Aesthetics so it stays with skin and tissue quality.

The IRM difference

Fat transfer still follows the regenerative framework.

A beautiful graft on a body that is inflamed, depleted, or still smoking is a harder graft. The plan should fit the person, the tissue, and the timing.

Honest limits

This is not a guaranteed size or a permanent filler.

Fat transfer is individualized. Outcomes depend on anatomy, how much donor fat you have, how the graft is placed and protected, and how your tissue accepts it. IRM will not promise a cup size, a specific glute shape, or that every cell will stay.

If fat transfer is not the right tool — not enough donor fat, a goal that needs a different approach, or health factors that should come first — that conversation happens before a procedure is booked. Education first. Then a plan, if there is one.
