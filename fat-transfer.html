Fat transfer

Volume that still feels like you.

Fat transfer at Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM) uses your own fat to restore shape where volume has changed — after pregnancy, weight shifts, aging, or because you want a softer contour. The goal is fullness that belongs to your body, not a one-size surgical look.

Book NowExplore Breast Augmentation

Your tissue. Your contour.

Patients usually come in asking about the breast, the glute, the face, or the hands. Same idea — your own fat — different plan for each area.

The IRM lens

Fat is living tissue, not a shortcut.

When people say they want to look like themselves again, they are often talking about volume that left after nursing, weight change, or years of a body redistributing fat in ways they did not choose. Fat transfer moves your own tissue instead of placing a device or a manufactured gel.

Some of the transferred fat is expected to resorb. Early swelling is not the final result. Harvest quality, placement, blood supply, nicotine, nutrition, and recovery all influence how much stays.

Choose your focus

Breast, glute, face, or hands.

Start with the area you care about. Each uses your own fat. The amount, the preparation, and the recovery are not the same.

Breast Augmentation

Get your volume back — without putting a device in your body. Your own fat can restore softness and shape after pregnancy, weight change, or years of feeling deflated in clothes that otherwise still fit. It will feel like you when you move. It will not jump two cup sizes in one session, and not every cell stays. What it can do is bring back a chest that looks and feels like yours.

Glute Augmentation

Curve that belongs to your frame — not a filter, not a packaged BBL. Fat taken from the waist, flanks, or thighs can add shape where you want it and refine where you don’t. The result should look like you on your best day, not like someone else’s before-and-after. We will not sell a size your anatomy cannot support.

Facial Biofiller

Look rested. Not done. Cheeks, temples, and the area around the mouth lose padding long before you feel old. Your own fat, prepared finely for the face, can soften those hollows so people say you look well — not “who did your filler?” It cannot be dissolved like gel, and not all of it stays. What stays is yours.

Hand Rejuvenation

Stop hiding your hands. When the padding thins, veins and tendons tell a different age than your face. A small amount of your own fat can restore that cushion so your hands match the vitality you still feel — at dinner, on the steering wheel, when you reach for someone. Small area. Real difference. Still your tissue.

Fat transfer vs surgery

It is a procedure. It is not the same as implant surgery.

Most people are weighing three paths: do nothing, have traditional plastic surgery, or use their own fat. Those are not the same job.

Vs implants

What is different from breast implant surgery?

Implants add a manufactured device and a set size. Fat transfer adds your own tissue and a softer, usually more modest change. There is no implant to maintain or replace later — and no guarantee of a specific cup size. If you want a large, immediate size change, fat transfer may not be the right tool.

Vs OR BBL

What is different from a hospital or surgery-center BBL?

Large-volume gluteal surgery in an operating room is a different operation — different anesthesia, different volumes, different risk. Here the conversation is contour with your own fat, only when it can be done thoughtfully. We do not sell a packaged BBL look.

Vs filler

What is different from filler in a syringe?

Gel filler is a manufactured product. It can be dissolved. Fat transfer uses your own tissue. On the face, that fat is prepared more finely so it can be placed in small areas. It cannot be dissolved the way gel filler can, and not every bit of it stays.

Recovery

Is there downtime?

Yes. Donor sites are often sore like a deep bruise. The area that received fat needs protection so the graft can take a blood supply. Plan for swelling that hides the true result for weeks, not days.

What to expect

Consult, candidacy, then harvest and recovery.

Before

Consult and candidacy

The change you hope to see, where extra fat lives, nicotine, medications, prior surgery or imaging, weight stability, and whether the body needs support first.

During

Harvest and placement

Fat is harvested from one area, prepared, and placed where volume is wanted. Comfort measures depend on the individual plan.

After

Healing and graft take

Swelling, bruising, and soreness. Compression on donor areas, no pressure on the graft, and activity limits while fat tries to live.

Sequence

Settling and staging

Week-one photos are swelling. The result you keep appears after fluid leaves and some fat does not survive. A second session is sometimes wiser.

Questions patients actually ask

Experience, limits, and what the days after feel like.

Does it hurt?

Harvest sites usually feel more tender than the area receiving fat. Most people describe soreness, tightness, and bruising. Comfort measures are discussed before the procedure.

How long until I can work, walk, or exercise?

Many people return to desk work within a few days if they can protect the grafted area. Walking is usually encouraged early. Impact exercise, heavy lifting, and pressure on the graft wait longer.

What will I look like at one week vs three months?

Week one is swelling. Friends may think it is done. It is not. The result you keep is closer to what you see after settling.

What if I do not have much extra fat?

Then fat transfer may not be the honest option, or the change may be smaller than you hoped. Better to hear that in consult than harvest from a body that cannot spare the graft.

Can I still get mammograms after breast fat transfer?

Yes. Fat grafting does not replace screening. Tell the imaging team you have had fat transfer. Oil cysts or small calcifications can appear and are why that history matters.

I do not want implants, but I miss the volume I used to have.

That is a common reason people ask about breast fat transfer. Next is whether you have enough donor fat and whether a modest, natural change matches the mirror you want.

Honest limits

This is not a guaranteed size or a permanent filler.

Outcomes depend on anatomy, donor fat, placement, aftercare, and how your tissue accepts the graft. IRM will not promise a cup size, a specific glute shape, or that every cell will stay.
