The IRM lens

Fat is living tissue, not a shortcut.

When people say they want to look like themselves again, they are often talking about volume that left after nursing, weight change, or years of a body redistributing fat in ways they did not choose. Fat transfer moves your own tissue instead of placing a device or a manufactured gel. Patients also like the other half of the work: the area the fat comes from is refined at the same time. Contour where you have extra. Volume where you want it. One procedure. Two changes.

We use Cellmyx AcquiCell with Lipo-Loop — a closed system designed to keep harvested fat viable. Research on this platform reports about 90% cell viability. That is why we chose it. It is not a promise that the size you see at week one is the size you keep. Swelling still leaves. Nicotine, nutrition, blood supply, and how you protect the graft still decide how much of that viable fat lives in you.

Choose your focus

Breast, glute, face, or hands.

Start with the area that would let you feel amazing in your body again. Each uses your own fat. The amount, the preparation, and the recovery are not the same.

Breast Augmentation

Still your tissue.

Fat transfer vs surgery

It is a procedure. It is not the same as implant surgery.

Most people are weighing three paths: do nothing, have traditional plastic surgery, or use their own fat. Those are not the same job.

Vs implants

What is different from breast implant surgery?

Implants add a manufactured device and a set size. Fat transfer adds your own tissue and a softer, usually more modest change. There is no implant to maintain or replace later — and no guarantee of a specific cup size. If you want a large, immediate size change, fat transfer may not be the right tool.

Vs OR BBL

What is different from a hospital or surgery-center BBL?

Large-volume gluteal surgery in an operating room is a different operation — different anesthesia, different volumes, different risk. Here the conversation is contour with your own fat, only when it can be done thoughtfully. We do not sell a packaged BBL look.

Vs filler

What is different from filler in a syringe?

Gel filler is a manufactured product. It can be dissolved. Fat transfer uses your own tissue. On the face, that fat is prepared more finely so it can be placed in small areas. It cannot be dissolved the way gel filler can, and not every bit of it stays.

Recovery

Is there downtime?

Yes. Donor sites are often sore like a deep bruise. The area that received fat needs protection so the graft can take a blood supply. Plan for swelling that hides the true result for weeks, not days.

Before you book

The questions people search at 11 p.m.

Feeling like yourself is the point. These are the facts that let you decide whether this tool matches your body.

How much change is realistic?

For the breast, one session is typically a modest step — about half to one cup — not two cups overnight. The glute and face follow the same rule: what your donor fat and tissue can support. If you want a dramatic, one-and-done size jump, fat transfer is the wrong tool.

How much of the fat stays?

The AcquiCell Lipo-Loop system used here is studied for high graft viability — about 90% in the research on this platform. That is better than older, open handling of fat. It is still not a guaranteed cup size. Swelling leaves. A second session can still be the wiser path if you want more than one honest pass can give.

Scars, feel, and later weight

Access points are small — not a fold scar from an implant pocket. Surviving fat should feel like you. It will also follow your weight: lose a lot later and some volume can leave; gain and it can grow. That is your tissue, not a device with a fixed size.

Cost, photos, and the consult

Pricing is not listed on this site. It depends on the area, the harvest, and whether a second session is wise. That number belongs in a conversation about your body. Bring the change you hope to see. Ask to see results that look like you — not a filter.

What to expect

What the days actually feel like.

Before

Consult and candidacy

Bring the change you hope to see — a photo, a dress that used to fit, the place on your body that no longer matches how you feel. We look at donor fat, nicotine, medications, prior surgery or imaging, and whether your weight and health can support a graft. If fat transfer is not the honest tool, you will hear that here. That conversation is the complimentary consult.

During

Harvest and placement

Fat is taken from an area that has extra — often the abdomen, flanks, or thighs — prepared, and placed where you want volume. That harvest is not wasted work. It is the contouring half of the procedure: a little less there, a little more here. Comfort measures are part of the plan. This is still a procedure — not a lunch-hour syringe of gel.

After

Healing and graft take

Expect soreness at the harvest site like a deep bruise, tightness where fat was placed, swelling, and bruising. Compression belongs on donor areas. Pressure does not belong on the graft — no sleeping on the breasts, no sitting hard on a fresh glute graft, no gripping if the hands were treated. Walking is usually encouraged. Impact exercise and heavy lifting wait. The fat is trying to grow a blood supply. Protect it.

Sequence

Settling and staging

Week one looks bigger than the result you keep. Friends may think it is done. It is swelling. Over the following weeks fluid leaves and some fat does not survive. That is expected biology, not failure. The result you live in shows after settling. A second session is sometimes the wiser path to the shape you actually want — not a promise we load onto day one.

Questions patients actually ask

Experience, limits, and what the days after feel like.

Does it hurt?

Harvest sites usually feel more tender than the area receiving fat. Most people describe soreness, tightness, and bruising. Comfort measures are discussed before the procedure.

How long until I can work, walk, or exercise?

Many people return to desk work within a few days if they can protect the grafted area. Walking is usually encouraged early. Impact exercise, heavy lifting, and pressure on the graft wait longer.

What will I look like at one week vs three months?

Week one is swelling. Friends may think it is done. It is not. The result you keep is closer to what you see after settling.

What if I do not have much extra fat?

Then fat transfer may not be the honest option, or the change may be smaller than you hoped. Better to hear that in consult than harvest from a body that cannot spare the graft.

Can I still get mammograms after breast fat transfer?

Yes. Fat grafting does not replace screening. Tell the imaging team you have had fat transfer. Oil cysts or small calcifications can appear and are why that history matters.

I do not want implants, but I miss the volume I used to have.

That is a common reason people ask about breast fat transfer. Next is whether you have enough donor fat and whether a modest, natural change matches the mirror you want.

How much bigger can I get in one session?

Think modest. For the breast, many people are in the half-to-one-cup range per session — not a two-cup implant look. Face and hands use smaller volumes on purpose. The glute is limited by the fat you actually have and by safety, not by a photo you saved.

How much of the transferred fat stays?

With the AcquiCell Lipo-Loop system we use, research reports about 90% cell viability — more of the graft is alive when it is placed. Week-one size is still swelling. What lives is living fat and will follow your weight. A second session is sometimes how people reach the shape they actually wanted.

Will I have scars?

Small access points at the harvest sites and where fat is placed. Not a breast-fold implant scar. They usually fade. Donor areas can feel more bruised than the area that received fat.

What if I gain or lose weight later?

Surviving fat is yours. Significant weight loss can shrink the result. Significant gain can enlarge it. Stable weight is part of keeping the contour you came for.

How much does it cost?

We do not publish a menu price. The plan depends on the area, how much harvest is involved, and whether staging makes sense. Cost is discussed in the complimentary consult — after we know whether you are a candidate.

Can I see before-and-after photos?

Ask in consult. You should see results on bodies that resemble yours, not only a highlight reel. We would rather show you an honest case than a filter.

Do I get contouring where the fat comes from?

Yes — that is the other half patients ask about. The harvest is liposuction with a purpose. Abdomen, flanks, or thighs can look more refined while the breast, glute, face, or hands gain volume. It is not a full body-lift. It is a two-for-one when you have extra fat to spare.
