Where is Idaho Regenerative Medicine located?

We are at 5513 N Glenwood St., Suite A & C, Garden City, ID 83714 — centrally located in the Treasure Valley, just minutes from downtown Boise.

Do you serve patients outside of Boise?

Yes. We regularly see patients from Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Caldwell, Kuna, and across the Treasure Valley. Virtual appointments are also available for appropriate visits.

What is the first step for new patients?

Most new patients begin with a Get to Know You Consult — a relationship-first conversation to understand your story, goals, and whether IRM is the right fit. Learn about becoming a new patient.

Is regenerative medicine covered by insurance?

IRM is a cash-pay clinic. We can provide documentation for HSA/FSA use when eligible. Pricing is discussed during your consult based on your individualized plan.
