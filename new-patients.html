New patients welcome

Your first step is a conversation — not a commitment.

Most new patients at Idaho Regenerative Medicine begin with a Get to Know You Consult. It is a relationship-first visit designed to hear your story, clarify your goals, and decide together whether IRM is the right place for your next step.

Book the Consult Call 208-370-2380
  • Doctor-led, naturopathic care
  • In-person or virtual options
  • No referral required

You do not need to know which therapy you want.

Many patients arrive after years of partial answers, normal labs, or treatments that helped briefly but did not hold. The consult is where we listen for the pattern and map what may come next.

How it works

Three steps to getting started.

Step 01

Book the Get to Know You Consult

Schedule online or call the office. Choose in-person at our Garden City clinic or a virtual visit when appropriate.

Step 02

Share your story

Bring your symptoms, timeline, prior care, labs, imaging, and what you hope health will make possible again. We listen for the pattern.

Step 03

Clarify the next step

You leave with a clearer understanding of how IRM thinks, whether we are the right fit, and what assessment or care may make sense next.

What to expect

A consult built around you.

The Get to Know You Consult is not a sales pitch. It is a focused conversation to understand what has been happening, what has been tried, and what matters most to you.

  • Time to be heardYour story, timeline, and priorities come first.
  • How IRM thinksWe introduce our four-phase framework so you understand why sequence matters.
  • Honest fit assessmentRegenerative care works best with aligned expectations, readiness, and partnership.
  • Clear next stepsIf IRM is a fit, we discuss what assessment or care planning may come next.

Practical details

Before your first visit.

Location

Garden City clinic

5513 N Glenwood St., Suite A & C, Garden City, ID 83714 — centrally located in the Treasure Valley, minutes from Boise. View location details.

Virtual option

Telehealth available

Some new patient consults can be done virtually. Learn about virtual appointments.

Payment

Cash-pay clinic

IRM is a cash-pay practice. HSA/FSA may apply when eligible. Pricing is discussed during your consult based on your individualized plan.

Records

Bring what you have

Prior labs, imaging reports, medication lists, and notes from other providers help us understand your history. Nothing is required to book.

Do I need a referral to become a patient?

No referral is required. You can book the Get to Know You Consult directly online or by calling 208-370-2380.

How long is the first consult?

The Get to Know You Consult is a focused visit designed to hear your story and clarify fit. Duration varies based on complexity, but plan for a thorough conversation.

What if I am not sure IRM is right for me?

That is exactly what the consult is for. There is no obligation to proceed with care. The goal is clarity and fit.

Can I book other appointment types as a new patient?

Most new patients start with the Get to Know You Consult. Other scheduling options are available for established patients or specific situations. View all scheduling options.
