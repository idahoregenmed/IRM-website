Total Nervous System Reset™

Help the body power down, release the load, and reconnect.

When the autonomic nervous system has been stuck in stress, threat, pain, or overwhelm, the body can have a hard time sleeping, regulating, healing, or receiving deeper care. Total Nervous System Reset™ is built on neural therapy and Autonomic Response Testing to help identify interference fields that may be draining the body’s vital force.

Find the interference. Support the signal.

This work uses Autonomic Response Testing to help guide neural therapy, including support for scars, teeth, tonsils, fascia, nerves, autonomic ganglia, and other interference fields when clinically appropriate.

When the system is stuck

The body can keep running a stress program long after the original trigger has passed.

Some patients do not feel “just stressed.” They feel wired, guarded, exhausted, reactive, unable to sleep, unable to settle, or trapped in a loop of pain and tension. For others, complex illness has left the body so sensitive that even supportive care can feel difficult to tolerate.

The reset concept is simple: identify where the system is being drained, then support the autonomic nervous system so the body has a better chance to reconnect, restore rhythm, and move out of constant defense. Patients may describe this type of work as cathartic, emotional, euphoric, deeply relieving, or like their body finally exhaled.

Patients often recognize themselves here

Stress, sleep, pain, overwhelm, and complex patterns often share a nervous system thread.

Stress & anxiety

Your body cannot find the off switch.

You may feel vigilant, restless, reactive, panicky, or unable to fully relax even when your mind knows you are safe.

Sleep & burnout

You are exhausted, but still wired.

Burnout can feel like pushing through the day, collapsing at night, then waking up unrefreshed because the system never truly downshifted.

Pain & tension

The body keeps guarding.

Pain, muscle tension, jaw clenching, neck tension, headaches, or protective holding patterns may persist when the nervous system stays on alert.

Complex illness

The system feels easily overwhelmed.

When the body is carrying chronic stress, illness, trauma patterns, immune burden, or sensitivity, regulation may need to come before more aggressive repair work.

The clinical idea

A reset is not about forcing calm. It is about finding what keeps the system on alert.

In neural therapy, an interference field is an area of the body that may continue sending disruptive signals through the nervous system. A scar, dental issue, old injury, inflamed tissue, irritated nerve, or autonomic ganglia pattern may keep the body spending energy on protection instead of repair.

Dr. Walton uses Autonomic Response Testing as a way to listen to the body’s regulatory system and help identify which interference fields may be draining the person’s vital force, or life force. The goal is not to chase every symptom. The goal is to find the signals that may be keeping the system stuck.

This is not a promise that one treatment erases stress, trauma, pain, or complex illness. It is a targeted way to support the physiology that influences sleep, tension, threat response, pain amplification, emotional capacity, and readiness for deeper care.

Stellate ganglion block

Common questions

What patients often want to know.

Is this only for anxiety?

No. Anxiety may be one sign of nervous system overdrive, but patients may also seek this work for sleep disruption, burnout, pain tension, overwhelm, PTSD patterns, or complex illness.

What does “reset” mean?

Reset means supporting a shift in state. It does not mean erasing the past or promising a specific outcome. The goal is to help the body move out of chronic defense, release unnecessary guarding, and reconnect with regulation.

What is an interference field?

An interference field is an area that may keep sending disruptive signals through the nervous system. In neural therapy, these areas are considered because they may drain energy, disrupt regulation, or keep the body in a protective pattern.

What is Autonomic Response Testing?

Autonomic Response Testing is used to help identify priority areas of stress or interference in the body. At Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM), it helps guide where neural therapy may be most appropriate instead of treating everything the same way. Learn more about Autonomic Response Testing.

Can it help pain?

It may be considered when pain is influenced by guarding, autonomic tone, nerve irritation, muscle tension, or a stress response that keeps amplifying the signal.

Where does it fit in the IRM plan?

Sometimes it comes early, especially when the body is too overwhelmed to tolerate deeper care. Other times it supports orthopedic, metabolic, hormone, IV, EBOO, or regenerative treatment plans.

Will I feel emotional during or after treatment?

Some patients describe emotional release, deep relief, or fatigue afterward. Responses vary and are discussed as part of informed consent.

How many sessions are typical?

Some patients notice shifts after one session; others benefit from a series as interference fields are addressed over time.
