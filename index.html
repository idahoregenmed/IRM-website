You're not broken.
You're communicating.

Pain, fatigue, brain fog, weight that will not move, a knee that will not recover, or labs that look “normal” while you still feel off are not proof that you are failing. They may be signals from a body asking for something it is not getting.

Modern medicine can be brilliant in a crisis, but chronic patterns often need a wider lens. We are interested in why the signals appeared in the first place: inflammation, missing inputs, joints that never finished healing, hormones and metabolism under strain, or systems that fell out of conversation with each other.

  • Symptoms as signalInformation, not failure.
  • Terrain over targetRestore the soil before patching the leaf.
  • Repair is realThe body has pathways ✦ we support them.

Our approach

A whole-person framework for rebuilding from the root up.

Healing is not random, and care should not be either. We look at the sequence: what the body is signaling, what is limiting repair, and which therapies make sense for the person in front of us.

Regenerative therapies

Therapy categories that support the body's repair, not replace it.

IRM draws from a broad and evolving therapeutic toolkit. These are starting points, not a complete menu, and the actual plan is shaped around the person, the terrain, the timing, and the goal.

Regenerative Orthopedics

Learn more

A broad orthopedic category using in-office ultrasound assessment, ultrasound-guided injection therapies, shockwave, and rehabilitation support when appropriate for joints, tendons, ligaments, nerves, fascia, and muscle pain.

Common entry point: pain that keeps returning, instability, old injuries, or bodywork that only helps temporarily.

Biologics

Learn more

Platelet-rich plasma, stem cell therapies, exosomes, and related biologic tools considered across orthopedics, tissue repair, hair restoration, and regenerative aesthetics.

Useful to explore when the goal is deeper repair signaling rather than simply chasing symptoms.

Hormone Optimization

Learn more

Evaluation and support for hormonal rhythms that influence energy, sleep, mood, libido, cognition, thyroid and stress signaling, repair, and how resilient the body feels over time.

Patients often ask about this when energy, sleep, mood, strength, intimacy, or recovery no longer feel like themselves.

Metabolic Optimization

Learn more

A physiology-first approach for weight resistance, insulin signaling, inflammation, body composition, GLP support, energy, and long-term metabolic resilience.

For the person who is doing the work but feels like their metabolism is not responding.

IV Therapy

Learn more

Terrain-support therapies for hydration, nutrients, antioxidants, recovery, and the internal reserves needed for deeper repair. Ozone can be discussed when it fits the bigger plan.

This can support the internal reserves the body needs for recovery, resilience, and repair.

Ozone Therapy

Learn more

Oxygen-focused therapies including Major Auto Heme, Hemealumen with Major Auto Heme, EBOO, Prolozone, and targeted ozone use when appropriate in the care sequence.

Considered when oxygenation, circulation, redox signaling, inflammation, or whole-body support are part of the larger picture.

Total Nervous System Reset™

Learn more

Neural therapy guided by Autonomic Response Testing to help identify interference fields, support regulation, and reduce the nervous system drain that can keep the body stuck in overdrive.

For patterns where the nervous system may be holding the body in protection, guarding, or overdrive.

Rehabilitation

Learn more

Shockwave, Emsculpt Neo rehabilitation, exercise, and physical therapy support for fascia, muscle activation, neuromuscular control, and functional recovery.

Rehabilitation helps the body hold gains from regenerative care, metabolic optimization, and strength rebuilding.

Peptide Therapy

Learn more

Biological signaling tools that may support repair, recovery, metabolism, immune communication, hormone rhythm, performance, and resilience when they fit the bigger picture.

A cross-category tool for repair, recovery, metabolism, hormones, performance, and resilience.

Regenerative Aesthetics

Learn more

A biology-first therapy category for skin quality, collagen support, hair restoration, and natural rejuvenation that reflects IRM's deeper repair philosophy.

For patients who want outer vitality to reflect better tissue quality, collagen support, and deeper internal resilience.

Regenerative therapies are not a guarantee of any specific outcome. Outcomes depend on the individual, the clinical picture, and the patient's commitment to their health. Therapies are tools, chosen when appropriate as part of a complete plan and a real conversation about evidence, risks, and what success would look like for you.

Philosophy

Medicine is broken.

Modern medicine can be brilliant in a crisis. But people with chronic pain, fatigue, inflammation, hormone shifts, weight resistance, or slow recovery are often left feeling like their body is a collection of disconnected parts.
