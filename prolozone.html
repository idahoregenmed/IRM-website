Orthopedic ozone therapy

Prolozone is the orthopedic side of ozone therapy.

Prolozone is an evolution of prolotherapy. It uses the same regenerative orthopedic concept, with ozone gas added into areas of damage, irritation, or poor healing.

Learn About the ConsultRegenerative OrthopedicsOzone Therapy

Nutrients, oxygen, and blood.

Damaged tissue needs the right repair environment. Prolozone is one form of ozone therapy, focused locally on orthopedic tissue when clinically appropriate.

How Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM) thinks

An evolution of prolotherapy.

At IRM, prolozone may be considered when tissue appears irritated, under-recovered, or when bony irregularities and deeper joint patterns are part of the picture.

The injection itself creates a controlled healing signal. The dextrose-based solution provides a nutrient signal, calms surrounding tissue, and may stimulate fibroblasts to lay down new collagen.

The ozone adds an oxygen-based stimulus that may support oxidative signaling, cellular cleanup, and local repair activity. After ozone reacts in tissue, oxygen remains available to the local environment.

Clinical context

A focused therapy inside a bigger plan.

This therapy is selected within a bigger orthopedic and whole-person plan. The best starting point is understanding the tissue target, the repair environment, and the sequence of care.

What to expect

For patients considering prolozone.

Prolozone combines prolotherapy principles with ozone. Here is what patients should know before booking.

Before

Clear orthopedic rationale

Assessment helps confirm whether oxygen-focused local signaling fits your tissue pattern and whether prolozone is the right tool.

During

Injection with ozone

Like prolotherapy, injections target specific tissue — with ozone gas added as part of the strategy for select cases.

After

Recovery window

Soreness and activity modifications are similar to prolotherapy. Your provider explains what to expect for your specific area.

Sequence

Part of a plan

Prolozone may be paired with stability work, PRP, rehabilitation, or terrain support — rarely used in isolation.

Is prolozone safe?

When used by a trained provider in appropriate clinical contexts, prolozone follows the same careful target selection as other orthopedic injections. Risks and alternatives are discussed individually.

How is prolozone different from IV ozone?

Prolozone is a local orthopedic injection. Systemic ozone therapies like Major Auto Heme or EBOO work differently. Learn about ozone therapy options.

Who is a good candidate?

Candidacy depends on exam findings, tissue targets, chronicity, and overall health terrain — clarified during consult and assessment.

Is prolozone the same as prolotherapy?

No. It builds on the prolotherapy concept but adds ozone gas as part of the injection strategy.

Why use ozone in orthopedic care?

Ozone may help amplify repair signaling and oxygen-focused tissue response when it fits the assessment and treatment plan.

How do I know if this belongs in my plan?

The starting point is the Get to Know You Consult. If IRM may be a fit, the next step may include assessment, orthopedic exam, ultrasound imaging when appropriate, and a personalized care discussion.
