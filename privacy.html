Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This website is operated by Natural Healing Dynamics LLC, doing business as Idaho Regenerative Medicine ("we" or "us"). We respect your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

What you send us: When you request a complimentary consultation or contact us, we collect what you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, and whether you agree to be contacted by text or email.

How you found us: We may record the page you came from and the tracking tags in the link you clicked (for example, from an ad or QR code) so we know which outreach is working.

Basic technical data: Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services listed below may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you visit.

Cookies: Some third-party features on this site, such as embedded forms, reviews, scheduling links, and web fonts, may set cookies or use similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Please don't send health information

This website is not a patient portal. Please do not include medical history, symptoms, or other health information in website forms, text messages, or email. If you have health questions, call our office.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request and schedule a complimentary consultation
  • To contact you by phone, email, or (only if you agree) text message about your request
  • To understand which of our outreach is working and to improve the website
  • To meet legal obligations

You can reply STOP to any text message to opt out of texts, or ask us at any time to stop contacting you.

We don't sell your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and respond to you, or when required by law.

Services we use

  • HighLevel (LeadConnector) stores consultation requests and helps us follow up by phone, email, and text.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • IntakeQ handles online booking when you use our scheduling links.
  • Elfsight displays Google reviews on our homepage.
  • Birdeye helps us manage online reviews, business listings, and social media posts, and may process your name and contact details if we ask you for a review.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare provide the website's typefaces.

Each of these companies has its own privacy policy.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us, but no website or internet transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the contact information you sent through this website by calling or emailing us.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
