What happens next

The right appointment creates a clearer path.

You do not need to diagnose yourself, choose a therapy, or know which part of your health story matters most before you schedule.

Some patients begin with a consult. Others are ready for a clinical visit. From there, IRM may recommend assessment, in-office ultrasound imaging, records review, advanced testing, regenerative therapies, foundational support, or a different next step based on the whole picture.

Decision guide

If you are choosing by goal, start here.

You do not have to know the exact therapy name to take the next step. Choose the closest starting point, and the visit can clarify what actually fits.

New to IRM

I want to know if this is the right fit.

Start with the Get to Know You Consult when you want orientation before choosing a clinical visit.

 Pain & function

I want help with pain, injury, or instability.

Choose regenerative orthopedics when the concern is joint, tendon, ligament, nerve, fascia, spine, or muscle pain.

 Metabolism

I feel stuck with weight, energy, or body composition.

Choose metabolic optimization when weight resistance, cravings, fatigue, inflammation, or insulin patterns are part of the story.

 Recovery

I am looking for IV Therapy or whole-body support.

Choose IV Therapy when you are looking for hydration, nutrients, antioxidants, recovery support, or internal replenishment.

Scheduling FAQ

Common booking questions.

I booked the wrong visit type — what should I do?

Call 208-370-2380 or email info@idahoregenmed.com as soon as possible. The office team can help you reschedule to the appropriate appointment.

How far out are appointments typically scheduled?

Availability varies by visit type and season. New patient consults may be available within days to a few weeks. Call the office for the current schedule.

What is your cancellation policy?

Contact the office as soon as you know you need to change your appointment. Policies may apply depending on notice given — the team can explain when you book.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is required. You can book directly online or by phone.

Can I schedule for a family member?

Yes, with appropriate authorization. Contact the office if you are scheduling on behalf of someone else so intake requirements are clear.

Where can I learn more before I book?

Start with New Patients, Get to Know You Consult, or Before Your Visit FAQ.
