How Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM) thinks

Looking beneath the cartilage.

Some joint pain patterns involve more than cartilage or soft tissue. The subchondral bone beneath the cartilage can be part of the orthopedic pain generator.

Intra-osseous injections are bone-targeted and may be discussed when symptoms, assessment, and imaging suggest deeper joint or bone involvement. Depending on the plan, ozone may be one of the tools considered for deeper bone-targeted work.

At IRM, this category is grouped with intra-articular injections because both require careful target selection and a clear orthopedic rationale.

Clinical context

A focused therapy inside a bigger plan.

This therapy is selected within a bigger orthopedic and whole-person plan. The best starting point is understanding the tissue target, the repair environment, and the sequence of care.

Target

Deeper bone patterns

Intra-osseous injections focus on bone-related structures rather than only the joint surface.

Selection

Used selectively

This is not a default therapy. It depends on the assessment and the clinical target.

Ozone

Bone-targeted ozone context

Ozone may be considered in select deeper orthopedic targets when the assessment supports that approach.

Context

Joint and bone group

It lives within the joint and bone-targeted branch of Regenerative Orthopedics.

What to expect

For patients considering intra-osseous injections.

Intra-osseous injections target deeper bone-related tissue. They are used selectively — not as a first-line option for every joint patient.

Before

Advanced assessment

Imaging, exam, and clinical rationale must support a bone-level target before this approach is considered.

During

Bone-targeted procedure

Injection is directed toward bone-related structures when the clinical picture points beyond cartilage or soft tissue alone.

After

Careful recovery

Post-procedure guidance is individualized. This is a more advanced target with specific activity and follow-up instructions.

Selective use

Not for everyone

Your provider explains why — or why not — this level of targeting fits your pattern.

When would bone-targeted injection be considered?

When symptoms, imaging, or exam suggest subchondral bone or deeper joint structures may be part of the pain generator.

Is this more invasive than a joint injection?

It targets a different structure and requires careful selection. Risks, benefits, and alternatives are discussed before proceeding.

Can ozone be used this way?

In select cases, yes — when assessment and sequence support deeper bone-targeted ozone work. Learn about ozone therapy.

Is this different from a joint injection?

Yes. Intra-articular means inside the joint. Intra-osseous means into bone-related tissue.

Why might bone matter in joint pain?

In some degenerative or chronic patterns, the deeper bone beneath the cartilage may contribute to pain and poor repair.

Can ozone be used intra-osseous?

Yes, in select cases. Ozone may be considered as part of deeper bone-targeted work when the assessment, target, and treatment sequence support that approach.

How do I know if this belongs in my plan?

The starting point is the Get to Know You Consult. If IRM may be a fit, the next step may include assessment, orthopedic exam, ultrasound imaging when appropriate, and a personalized care discussion.
