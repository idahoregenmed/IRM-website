Vitality Reserve™

A long-term container for preserving progress.

Vitality Reserve™ is being designed for patients who are ready to stay connected to their health, protect the gains they have made, and receive thoughtful regenerative support over time.

Learn About the ConsultBook Now

Care should have rhythm.

Healing is not always one appointment or one therapy. The right rhythm can help the body stabilize, repair, and keep moving forward without starting over each time something changes.

The idea

For patients who want continuity, not scattered care.

Vitality Reserve™ is not meant to be a generic wellness subscription. It is a continuity model for patients who benefit from ongoing strategy, periodic recalibration, and access to the therapies that support their larger health direction.

The goal is to make regenerative care feel more coherent: less guessing, less starting over, and more attention to the sequence that helps your body hold progress.

What it may support

A reserve for the systems that help you feel like yourself.

Vitality Reserve™ gives patients a way to build resources around the work of healing, recovery, strength, and long-term vitality.

Resilience

Staying ahead of depletion.

Support for patients who want to maintain energy, recovery, hormone rhythm, metabolic health, and stress capacity over time.

Repair

Protecting the gains.

Ongoing care may help patients keep building on progress after regenerative orthopedics, biologics, rehabilitation, or deeper terrain work.

Readiness

Better timing for deeper therapies.

Advanced therapies work best when the terrain is supported. Vitality Reserve™ can help patients stay more prepared for the next layer of care.

Longevity

Health as a living strategy.

The focus is not just symptom relief. It is capacity, function, vitality, and a body that feels supported through the next season of life.

Three reserve levels

Different seasons of life call for different levels of commitment.

Each level follows the same philosophy: create a dedicated reserve for care, stay more consistent with the work that matters, and make health easier to plan for over time.

Foundation Reserve

Begin funding your health.

A steady, accessible way to start building a personal health reserve. This level may fit patients who want more structure, want to maintain gains, or are ready to begin work they have been postponing.

  • Builds consistency without a large monthly commitment.
  • Creates a reserve that can be used toward eligible Idaho Regenerative Medicine (IRM) care.
  • Supports the first layer of momentum and follow-through.
Transformation Reserve

Stay in the work.

For patients who are actively rebuilding, recovering, or optimizing and want a stronger reserve to support that process. This is often the natural fit when someone is ready to stop improvising health from month to month.

  • Supports meaningful care over time instead of scattered decisions.
  • Creates more room for therapies, follow-up, and recalibration.
  • Helps patients stay engaged through the deeper layers of repair.
Optimization Reserve

Treat your health like your most important asset.

For patients taking a long view of vitality, performance, regeneration, and longevity. This level may fit those who want to protect capacity, use multiple therapies over time, and build health instead of reacting to decline.

  • Creates the most substantial reserve for ongoing strategy.
  • Supports advanced regenerative, metabolic, hormone, and longevity care.
  • Reflects a deeper commitment to preserving strength and capacity.

Specific reserve terms, eligible services, and member details can be reviewed with the IRM team so the structure matches the patient’s season, priorities, and care plan.

Who it may be for

Not everyone needs a reserve. Some patients are ready for one.

A good fit when

You want a long-term health partner.

  • You want to preserve progress instead of waiting until things fall apart.
  • You are actively working on hormones, metabolism, repair, inflammation, recovery, or longevity.
  • You value strategy, education, and thoughtful sequencing.
  • You want your care to feel integrated instead of pieced together.
Maybe not yet when

The first step is still clarity.

  • You are brand new to IRM and still need a Get to Know You Consult.
  • You need assessment before a long-term rhythm makes sense.
  • You are looking for one isolated therapy without a broader care relationship.
  • You want pricing before the clinical direction is clear.

Begin

Start with fit, then build the rhythm.

If Vitality Reserve™ sounds like the kind of long-term support you want, the next step is still a conversation. IRM can help you understand whether this kind of care belongs in your next season.
