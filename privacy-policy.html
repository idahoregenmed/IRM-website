Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Overview

Idaho Regenerative Medicine ("IRM," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website at idahoregenmed.com, contact our office, or use our online scheduling tools.

Information we collect

Website usage: We may collect standard web analytics data such as pages visited, browser type, device type, referring URL, and general location (city/region level) through our hosting provider.

Contact information: When you call, email, or submit a form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, and the content of your message.

Scheduling: Online appointment booking through IntakeQ may collect personal and health-related information necessary to schedule and prepare for your visit. IntakeQ maintains its own privacy policy governing data collected through their platform.

Protected health information (PHI): Once you become a patient, your medical records are protected under HIPAA and applicable state privacy laws. This website privacy policy does not replace our Notice of Privacy Practices provided to patients.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule appointments
  • To provide and coordinate medical care for established patients
  • To improve our website and patient experience
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Third-party services

We use trusted third-party services that may process data on our behalf, including:

  • IntakeQ — online scheduling and patient intake forms
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Elfsight — Google Reviews widget (homepage)
  • Google Fonts / Fontshare — web typography

These providers have their own privacy policies. We select services that maintain reasonable security practices.

Cookies and tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Third-party widgets (such as the reviews widget) may set their own cookies.

Website content and AI usage

All content on this website — including text, frameworks, brand language, and design — is protected by copyright. Unauthorized copying, AI training use, dataset creation, automated replication, or derivative use is not permitted without written permission from Idaho Regenerative Medicine. See our llms.txt file for AI usage restrictions.

Data security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Patients may exercise HIPAA rights regarding their protected health information by contacting our office directly.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
